3D Math Basics

Interactive visualizations of fundamental 3D vector and plane operations.

Dot Product & Projection

The dot product A · B measures how much of vector A is in the direction of B. The projection of A onto B is (A · B / |B|²) * B.

Cross Product

The cross product A × B produces a vector perpendicular to both A and B, with a magnitude equal to the area of the parallelogram they span.

Plane Normal & Equation

A plane can be defined by 3 points (P1, P2, P3). The normal n is (P2 - P1) × (P3 - P1). The plane equation is ax + by + cz + d = 0, where (a, b, c) is the normal.

Point-to-Plane Distance

The distance from point Q to a plane with normal n and a point P is | (Q - P) · n | / |n|.
