Geometry & Graphics Algorithms

A collection of 3D, Computational Geometry, and Game Graphics visualizations using Canvas 2D.

3D Geometry

  • Cross & Dot Product / Plane Math
  • Ray-Sphere & Ray-Triangle (Möller–Trumbore)
  • 3D Convex Hull
  • AABB & OBB Collision

Computational Geometry

  • Closest Pair of Points (Divide & Conquer)
  • Line Sweep / Bentley–Ottmann
  • Voronoi Diagram & Delaunay Triangulation
  • Minkowski Sum

Game / Graphics Oriented

  • Frustum Culling
  • Quadtree & Octree
  • SAT (Separating Axis Theorem)
  • Bezier, B-Splines & NURBS
  • 4D & 5D Cubes
