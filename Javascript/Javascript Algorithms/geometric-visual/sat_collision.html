Separating Axis Theorem (SAT)

The Separating Axis Theorem (SAT) states that two convex shapes do not overlap if there exists an axis on which their projections do not overlap. For polygons, we only need to check axes perpendicular to the edges of the polygons.

Drag the shapes to see the collision detection and the projected axes in real-time. If they overlap on all axes, they are colliding (shown in red).
