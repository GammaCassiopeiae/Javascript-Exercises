d/dx(xⁿ) = n·x^(n−1) d/dx(x³) = 3x²

192. Product Rule

Calculus
d/dx(uv) = u'v + uv' Derivative of a product

193. Quotient Rule

Calculus
d/dx(u/v) = (u'v−uv') / v² Derivative of a ratio

194. Chain Rule

Calculus
d/dx[f(g(x))] = f'(g(x))·g'(x) Derivative of a composite

195. Derivative of e^x

Calculus
d/dx(eˣ) = eˣ The exponential is its own derivative

196. Derivative of ln(x)

Calculus
d/dx(ln x) = 1/x The natural log's derivative

197. Derivative of sin(x)

Calculus
d/dx(sin x) = cos x Sine's derivative is cosine

198. Derivative of cos(x)

Calculus
d/dx(cos x) = −sin x Cosine's derivative is negative sine

199. Fundamental Theorem of Calculus

Calculus
∫ₐᵇ f(x)dx = F(b)−F(a) Links derivatives and integrals

200. Power Rule (Integral)

Calculus
∫ xⁿ dx = x^(n+1)/(n+1) + C Antiderivative of powers

201. Integral of 1/x

Calculus
∫ (1/x) dx = ln|x| + C Special case (n=−1)

202. Integral of e^x

Calculus
∫ eˣ dx = eˣ + C Exponential is its own integral

203. Integration by Parts

Calculus
∫ u dv = uv − ∫ v du Reverses the product rule

204. Probability of Event

Probability
P(A) = Favorable / Total Roll a 6 on a die: P = 1/6

205. Complementary Event

Probability
P(A') = 1 − P(A) Not rolling a 6: P = 5/6

206. Independent Intersection

Probability
P(A∩B) = P(A)·P(B) Two independent events both occurring

207. Conditional Probability

Probability
P(A|B) = P(A∩B) / P(B) Probability of A given B occurred

208. Bayes' Theorem

Probability
P(A|B) = P(B|A)·P(A) / P(B) Reverses conditional probability

209. Mean (Average)

Statistics
μ = Σxᵢ / n Sum of values divided by count

210. Variance

Statistics
σ² = Σ(xᵢ−μ)² / n Average squared deviation from mean

211. Standard Deviation

Statistics
σ = √σ² Spread in same units as data

Complex Numbers

Complex Numbers

Complex Numbers

Complex Numbers
